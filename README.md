# DigiShift — SME Digital Transformation Advisor

DigiShift is a strategic modernization engine engineered specifically for traditional small and medium-sized enterprises (custom machine shops, field HVAC contractors, building merchants, multi-venue catering, and private clinics). 

It replaces costly, months-long management consulting engagements with an immediate, algorithmic diagnostic that quantifies operational friction, scores organizational maturity, and models financial ROI.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

- **5-Pillar Digital Maturity Diagnostic**:
  - Scores Customer & Sales Intake, Operations & Fulfillment, Financials & Cashflow, Workforce Collaboration, and Data & Cyber Hygiene.
  - Benchmarked against calibrated SME peer cohorts with dual polygonal radar and grouped bar charts.

- **Baseline vs. Projected Comparison Chart**:
  - Live horizon modeling (Post-Phase 1 Quick Wins vs. Post-Phase 2 Integrations vs. Full Automation).
  - Displays capability progression, peer-beating milestones, and point-lift envelopes.

- **Algorithmic Bottleneck Priority Ranker**:
  - Quantifies annual financial drain: `Wasted Hours × Blended Labor Rate ($/hr) + Rework Error Rates + Revenue Leakage`.
  - Filterable by department with 1-click hour re-estimation and custom bottleneck creation.

- **Phased Adoption Roadmap (14-Month Timeline)**:
  - Phase 1: Foundation & Digital Invoicing (Quick Wins).
  - Phase 2: Operations, Inventory Barcoding & Shopfloor Travelers.
  - Phase 3: Connected Cloud ERP & Automated Analytics.
  - Generates realistic SaaS stack recommendations (ShopVox, Jobber, Fishbowl, Zapier, QuickBooks Online).

- **Financial Impact & Payroll Recapture Calculator**:
  - Real-time sensitivity sliders for blended staff wages ($18–$75/hr) and productivity capture factors.
  - Computes net 3-year EBITDA savings, payback period (in months), and SaaS subscription amortizations.

- **AI Executive Advisor (Gemini 3.8 Flash)**:
  - Context-aware chatbot providing domain-specific answers grounded in the SME's profile and active bottlenecks.
  - On-demand executive briefing memos formatted for board and CEO review.

- **Executive Dossier Export Engine**:
  - Direct 1-click standalone HTML report generation and clean, print-optimized PDF dossier formatting.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion, Lucide Icons
- **AI Intelligence**: Google Gemini 3.8 Flash (`@google/genai`)
- **Backend**: Node.js & Express server-side proxy
- **Security & Preferences**: Role-based profiles, dark/light modes, brute-force rate-limiting, and laptop viewport density auto-scaling
